Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 2026 • Estimated Reading Time: 3 mins • Plain English Policy

At SCHÖN Interiors ("Interiors by Arun Reddy"), we believe in transparency, craftsmanship, and respecting your personal privacy. This document outlines how our website operates, what data we interact with, and our commitments to you.

🛡️ Privacy in Brief

We respect your time and privacy. Here is the short version of our commitments:

  • No Tracking Cookies: We do not use third-party advertising cookies or cross-site tracking.
  • No Selling of Data: We never sell, rent, or trade your personal information.
  • Minimal Collection: We only collect information you deliberately provide when contacting us.
  • Transparent Assets: We openly disclose all external fonts, CDNs, and media services we use.

1. Who We Are

SCHÖN Interiors is an interior design studio based in Hyderabad, India, led by Arun Reddy. We specialize in luxury residential interiors, bespoke modular kitchens, custom furniture, wall design, and architectural lighting solutions.

This Privacy Policy applies solely to our website (schoninteriors.com and its official pages).

2. Information We Collect

We collect information in two simple ways:

A. Information You Voluntarily Provide

When you reach out to us for a design consultation or inquiry via email, phone, or direct messaging, you may share:

  • Your name and contact details (phone number, email address).
  • Your property location, floor plans, design preferences, and estimated budget.
  • Any additional notes or design references you choose to send.

B. Automated Technical Information

Like nearly all websites, our hosting servers automatically register standard technical request logs whenever a page is visited. This includes your general Internet Protocol (IP) address, browser type, operating system, and the time of your visit. This data is used strictly for network security, diagnostics, and maintaining site availability.

4. Browser Storage & Cookies

No Advertising or Tracking Cookies

We do not use third-party marketing cookies, tracking pixels, or cross-site behavioral monitoring tools on this website.

Session Storage Usage

We use a single client-side browser key via the standard Web Storage API:

  • sessionStorage.getItem('introPlayed'): Stores a temporary flag during your browsing session to remember if the introductory SCHÖN logo animation has already finished, preventing repetitive animations when navigating between pages.

This session data remains entirely on your personal device and automatically clears when you close your browser tab.

5. How We Use Your Information

Any information you provide to SCHÖN Interiors is used exclusively to:

  • Respond to your interior design inquiries, consultations, and quote requests.
  • Prepare customized design proposals, moodboards, and material estimates.
  • Coordinate site visits, material selections, and on-site project execution.
  • Maintain our business records and client communications.

We will never sell, monetize, or distribute your private contact details to third-party telemarketers or advertisers.

6. Data Security & Retention

We implement standard physical, technical, and organizational safeguards to protect your personal information against unauthorized access, loss, or misuse. Our site is delivered over secure HTTPS encryption.

We retain project inquiries and client communications only for as long as necessary to fulfill project requirements, ongoing warranty/support commitments, or statutory legal obligations.

7. Your Rights & Choices

You have full control over your personal information. At any time, you may:

  • Request a copy of the personal information or project notes we have on file for you.
  • Request corrections or updates to your contact details.
  • Request that we delete your contact information and consultation records from our active databases.

To exercise any of these rights, simply email us at hello@schoninteriors.com with your request.

8. Contact Us & Updates

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect evolving site features or legal standards. Any updates will be published immediately on this page with a revised "Last Updated" date.
